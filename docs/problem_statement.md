# The problem this project solves

**Why does buying a home still feel out of reach?**

The technical focus is an audit of the evidence behind a claim that renters are ready to buy. A banking or financial-reporting analyst should check the meaning of the measures, the observation period and purchase-cost assumptions before using such a claim.

## A concrete example

Reported renter net saving was positive CAD 269 in 2025Q4. It included a CAD 841 pension adjustment, while income less consumption was negative CAD 572. It does not measure verified money available for a deposit.

The sum of reported net saving across all four quarters was negative CAD 557 in 2025. Treating one positive quarter as a positive annual saving rate would give a different and unsupported story.

An illustrative CAD 600,000 purchase requires CAD 35,000 minimum down under the current tiers, not CAD 30,000. A separately assumed closing-cost allowance must be shown as an assumption.

## The solution

A reproducible audit pipeline that reconciles the saving figures, checks complete periods, applies the price-tier rule and records whether drafted claims are supported, incorrect or missing evidence. A three-page Power BI report makes the trail visible and lets the reader inspect purchase-cash scenarios.

The audit action is to correct unsupported wording and request verified funds, a full purchase budget and eligibility evidence. The project does not approve borrowers or infer a personal buying date from public averages.
