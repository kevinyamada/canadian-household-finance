Canadian Household Finance / An audit project

Why does buying a home still feel out of reach?

A savings headline can make buying look closer than the evidence supports. I audited the figures and upfront costs behind a claim that renters are ready to buy.

The finding: reported saving is not proof of money available for a deposit.

Start with the saving figure.

Renter averages · 2025

In the final quarter of 2025, renters showed positive net saving of CAD 269. But that figure included a pension adjustment.

Income was below consumption. The adjustment made reported saving positive; it did not establish cash available for a deposit.

Adding the four actual quarters gives −CAD 557 of reported net saving for the full year.

Then check the purchase budget.

An explicit scenario

For an illustrative CAD 600,000 home, “5% down” gives the wrong minimum. The rule applies different rates to different parts of the price.

The correct minimum is CAD 35,000. Adding a separately assumed closing-cost allowance changes the cash scenario again.

Before calling someone ready to buy, verify their available funds, complete purchase budget and mortgage eligibility.

What the audit changes

Correct the claim before using it for a decision.

I reconciled the saving figures, checked the time periods and purchase-price rules, and recorded which conclusions need more evidence. The output is a traceable review, rather than a buying date inferred from averages.

The claim register uses deliberately drafted examples. It does not allege errors in Statistics Canada's published reporting.

How it was built

Python in VS Code checks the source measures. SQL independently reconciles annual totals. Power BI shows the evidence and assumptions.

Public group averages cannot establish an individual buyer's finances. The audit examines one part of the homebuying problem; it does not measure all causes of housing affordability or predict prices.

Sources & scope

Statistics Canada income, consumption and saving, 2020–2025. Purchase rules: CMHC and Finance Canada.

The repository preserves source snapshots, checks and assumptions. Earlier forecasting and GPU studies remain supporting work. This is an independent project; no TD customer data is used.
