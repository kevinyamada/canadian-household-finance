Canadian Household Finance

What is putting pressure on Canadian households?

Debt payments kept rising. Income grew faster, so the national payment ratio fell. Household group averages show why that headline needs context.

A project using public Statistics Canada data, Python, SQL and Power BI.

01 / National · 2026 Q2

Income grew faster than debt payments.

Required payments rose 0.97%. Income rose 2.06%. That brought debt service down to 14.52% of income before interest.

02 / Household groups · 2025 Q4

Mortgage owners had more debt relative to income.

Debt was 306.1% of annualized disposable income for mortgage owners, compared with 56.4% for renters.

How I built it

From public data to a report someone can check.

Python in VS Code: Cleaned Statistics Canada tables, checked accounting identities and compared forecasts in time order.

SQL: Joined quarterly data and compared changes within household groups.

Power BI Desktop: Built eight pages for findings, group comparisons, reporting checks, models and payment scenarios.

The project includes 936 group-quarter rows, 146 national quarters, 32 passing local tests and 71 source-report schema checks. Saved Modal H100 experiments are documented in the repo.
