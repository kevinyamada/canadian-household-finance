# Housing-readiness audit

**Why does buying a home still feel out of reach?**

## Audit conclusion

Reported saving does not establish money available for a down payment.

The sum of reported renter net saving in 2025 was negative CAD 557 per household.

In 2025Q4, reported renter net saving was CAD 269, made up of income less consumption of CAD -572 plus a pension adjustment of CAD 841. A positive quarter does not establish cash available for a home purchase.

## What a purchase would need

For an illustrative CAD 600,000 home, the current minimum down payment is CAD 35,000. A separately assumed 2.5% closing-cost allowance adds CAD 15,000, for an upfront cash scenario of CAD 50,000. This is not an observed national home price, complete closing statement or mortgage approval.

## The audit action

Verify liquid funds and the purchase budget before making a readiness claim. Do not turn group net saving into a personal buying date.

The claim register contains deliberately drafted examples. Unsupported interpretations are sent for evidence or correction; no fraud or misstatement by Statistics Canada is alleged.

## Limits

- These are averages over changing household groups, not a panel of individual renters.
- Consumption contains national-accounts imputations; income less consumption is not bank-account cash flow.
- Annual values sum the four published quarterly per-household estimates; minor rounding differences are retained.
- Home prices and closing-cost allowances in the cash check are explicit scenarios.
- The cash check does not include mortgage approval, all taxes, loan insurance or a complete legal closing statement.
